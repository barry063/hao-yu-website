01 / Profile

Connecting growth,
structure and response.

I am a PhD researcher in the Hofmann Group at Cambridge, working on tungsten oxides and transition-metal dichalcogenides. My doctoral research examines how source chemistry, source–substrate geometry and processing conditions influence material formation.

My approach combines controlled growth experiments with quantitative Raman/PL analysis and complementary evidence from collaborators. I also develop RamanPL_2D to turn spectroscopy datasets into fitted parameters, spatial maps and structured outputs.

Earlier work at Oxford connected photonic-device measurements with design and simulation. Alongside research, I have experience in technology assessment and deep-tech commercialisation.

02 / Research approach

Synthesis, measurement, analysis.

Vapour-phase synthesis

Close-space sublimation and oxide-to-sulphide conversion: relating precursor chemistry and confined transport to the morphology of low-dimensional materials.

Spectroscopy and characterisation

Raman/PL measurements and spatial mapping, interpreted alongside collaborative structural and chemical characterisation to connect processing with material response.

Research software and analysis

Python workflows for preprocessing, peak fitting, mapping and batch export of spectroscopy data, developed around experimental materials questions.

03 / Selected research

From materials growth to optical data.

Current doctoral work, research software
and earlier photonic-device research.

Feb 2023 – Jan 2025

Close-space growth of tungsten oxide and sulphide nanostructures

How does a confined vapour geometry influence oxide and sulphide nanostructure formation?

I designed and conducted CSS-facilitated CVD growth and Raman/PL measurements, and co-wrote the first-author paper with Stephan Hofmann. The study connects precursor delivery and growth conditions with material formation.

Operando SEM, TEM/EDX and XPS measurements in this study were performed by collaborators.

First-author paper · Nanoscale ↗

Jun 2025 – Apr 2026

NaCl-assisted close-space growth of WS₂

How can source treatment and source–substrate geometry guide planar WS₂ growth?

I investigated salt-assisted close-space growth using paired source–substrate analysis and Raman/PL measurements to examine precursor evolution alongside deposition outcomes.

This work forms part of my submitted thesis; a first-author manuscript is in preparation.

Jun – Aug 2025

Optical characterisation of directly written MoS₂

How can spectroscopy assess the layer structure and optical response of directly written 2D material?

I contributed Raman and photoluminescence measurements and analysis to a collaborative closed-loop crystal-layer writing study. The growth platform and complementary measurements were led by other co-authors.

The co-authored manuscript is under review.
